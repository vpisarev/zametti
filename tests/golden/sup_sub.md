# Superscript and subscript

Water is H<sub>2</sub>O and the area is x<sup>2</sup>; a note<sup>1</sup> without a footnote.

Inside **bold<sup>up</sup>** and _italic<sub>down</sub>_, and [a link<sup>2</sup>](http://example.org).

A lone <sup>tag without its pair stays text, as does an empty <sup></sup> pair.

Code keeps `<sup>x</sup>` as it is.
